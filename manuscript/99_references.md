# References

Every entry below is a real, checkable source and mirrors an entry in
`manuscript/references.bib` (same key, same bibliographic data). Cited keys
appear in the prose as `[@key]`.

[brandes]: U. Brandes, *A faster algorithm for betweenness centrality*,
Journal of Mathematical Sociology, 25(2), 163-177, 2001. — the
betweenness-centrality algorithm used for supply-chain choke points.

[hhi]: U.S. Department of Justice & Federal Trade Commission, *Horizontal
Merger Guidelines*, 2010. — the antitrust codification of the
Herfindahl-Hirschman index used as the concentration objective.

[christopher2004]: M. Christopher & H. Peck, *Building the Resilient Supply
Chain*, The International Journal of Logistics Management, 15(2), 1-14, 2004.
— supply-chain disruption and resilience framing for the cascade model.

[ast]: R. K. Ahuja, T. L. Magnanti & J. B. Orlin, *Network Flows: Theory,
Algorithms, and Applications*, Prentice Hall, 1993. — the graph reachability
and flow constructions underlying the cascade and the mine network.

[kreibich2008]: H. Kreibich & A. H. Thieken, *Assessment of damage caused by
high groundwater inundation*, Water Resources Research, 44(9), W09409, 2008.
— groundwater-inundation damage, the basis for the valley-impact coupling.

[greenswets1966]: D. M. Green & J. A. Swets, *Signal Detection Theory and
Psychophysics*, John Wiley and Sons, New York, 1966. — the detection-theory
framing of the cover-risk model.

[dixon]: M. J. Dixon & S. G. Coles, *Modelling Association Football Scores and
Inefficiencies in the Football Betting Market*, Journal of the Royal
Statistical Society: Series C (Applied Statistics), 46(2), 265-280, 1997. —
outcome models priced against a bookmaker's market.

[kelly1956]: J. L. Kelly, *A New Interpretation of Information Rate*, Bell
System Technical Journal, 35(4), 917-926, 1956. — the Kelly criterion used to
size the cover bankroll.

[film]: *A View to a Kill*, dir. J. Glen, Eon Productions, 1985 — the film
whose scheme this package formalizes (mission codename ZORIN).
